# TypeBrush — Week 3 SEO Review

This review plan details checks to evaluate impressions, query discovery, and landing page visibility from Day 15 to Day 21.

---

## 1. Review Checklist (Days 15 - 21)

- [ ] **Top Queries Identification**: List terms generating impressions and clicks.
- [ ] **Top Landing Pages Visibility**: Track click distributions across `/touch-typing`, `/typing-speed-test`, and `/wpm-calculator`.
- [ ] **Average CTR & Position**: Map average position benchmarks inside Search Console.
- [ ] **Content Opportunities Audit**: Identify secondary keywords in search results to inject into H2/H3 guides.
- [ ] **Internal Link Health Check**: Confirm all redirects and structural links are operational.

---

## 2. Target Objectives
- **Search Impressions**: Growing trend observed on GSC dashboards.
- **Query Discovery**: Identifying at least 15+ target keywords (e.g. "touch typing guide", "WPM calculations").
